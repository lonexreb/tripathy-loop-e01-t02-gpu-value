This project uses the `gpu-price-pack v0.1.1-handover` compilation prepared by Pacific Machines for the Iterate AI Research Hack under CC BY 4.0, subject to the per-source licences recorded by the pack.

The dataset itself is not redistributed through this repository. The local immutable pack contains the complete upstream attribution, licence captures, source register, transformation notes, and dropped-row record in `gpu-price-pack-v0.1.1-handover/ATTRIBUTION.md`, `licences/`, `sources_register.csv`, and `DATA.md`.

Forecast results must name the source, instrument class, benchmark contract, and relevant upstream attribution; they must not imply that posted prices are transactions or splice distinct instruments into one price curve.
